Part 2 · Recipes

2.3 Image generation recipes

Artifact-led

Case study covers, proof sections, portfolio. 45° overhead, f/2.8, background #f5f2eb, −15% saturation, paper wear visible, no people.

"Documentary photograph of printed kanban board with sticky notes on light oak table, natural soft window light upper left, 45-degree overhead, shallow DoF f/2.8, background #f5f2eb, desaturated -15%, handwritten annotations in blue ink, coffee cup partially visible, no people, 4:3"

Environmental

Process sections, team culture. Available light, candid, environment tells the story. No stock huddles, no eye contact, 35mm perspective.

"Documentary photography, person reviewing documents at standing desk, available natural light, candid, whiteboard with diagrams slightly out of focus, palette #fbfaf7 #f5f2eb, no smiling at camera, analog film aesthetic, 35mm"

Abstract / textural

Backgrounds, dividers, hero support. Paper, linen, stone, dried grass. −30% saturation, duotone warm gray, blur 20px when used behind text.

"Abstract macro photography, weathered paper texture with subtle grain, soft directional light, monochrome warm gray #4b555c tones, extreme close-up, tactile surface, fine art style, 1:1"
Negative prompt · all categories
stock photo, posed, pristine condition, studio lighting, overhead fluorescent, people, hands, smiling, corporate gloss, oversaturated, HDR effect, white background
Post-processing
crop 4:3 or 16:9
desaturate -10%
lift blacks slightly
add subtle grain

Mode switching · React context
const ColorModeContext = createContext('muted');

function ColorModeProvider({ children, mode = 'muted' }) {
  return (
    <ColorModeContext.Provider value={mode}>
      <div data-color-mode={mode}>{children}</div>
    </ColorModeContext.Provider>
  );
}

// Usage
<ColorModeProvider mode="muted">
  <LandingPage />
  <ColorModeProvider mode="ripe">
    <ResultsSection />
  </ColorModeProvider>
</ColorModeProvider>
